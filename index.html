P2P Transfers

Preview the transfer fee calculation. The fee is 10% of the amount the recipient receives. For example, a recipient amount of UGX 10,000 means a sender total of UGX 11,000. This calculator does not send money.

Real transfers require authenticated members, server-side fee calculations, balance checks, package eligibility, transaction limits and secure authorization.
