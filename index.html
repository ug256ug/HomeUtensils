Apply to become an agent

An agent must be an active Plus member and must submit an application. Plus membership alone does not make you an agent.

Starting capital: UGX 50,000.
After an administrator verifies the capital and approves your application, the agent float becomes UGX 57,500, including a UGX 7,500 bonus. This float is non-withdrawable and is restricted to facilitating package activation.

Your NIN is private and should only be accessible to you and authorised administrators. It will not be shown in public agent search results.
