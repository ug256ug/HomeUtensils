Important safety controls

This dashboard is currently read-only. It does not approve payments, alter balances, approve withdrawals, activate packages, send announcements or issue gift codes. Those actions require verified server-side functions and secure Firebase permissions.

Never share administrator passwords or private credentials. A successful dashboard sign-in does not by itself authorize every financial action.
